For vehicles driven by front wheels, when towing, lift the front wheels to the rear wheels, turn the ignition switch to "ACC" position, and
unlock the steering wheel so that the front wheels are in front. The steering wheel is locked by a special locking mechanism and a
hazard warning lamp is illuminated.

### Warning

Do not touch the front wheels during towing, otherwise the transmission may be damaged.

::: warning ВНИМАНИЕ
Caution The trailer distance shall not exceed 25 km and the trailer speed shall not exceed 40 km/h.
:::

### Warning

Always park the vehicle on a hard flat ground. If you want to jack up the vehicle on soft ground, you must place the load dispersing
block under the jack. Always place the stop in the opposite direction of the diagonal wheel position at the jacking point. Failure to
follow these instructions may result in personal injury.
When the vehicle needs to be repaired with a jack, it is essential to fully understand the following instructions. The correct support
points must be confirmed, usually in the open area between the front and rear wheels. When using a jack, use a gasket to protect the

Maintenance interval
Maintenance matters 23
Maintenance interval
Maintenance items
Do not start the engine where it is prohibited to start.
When it is necessary to start the engine for maintenance, the parking brake must be fully braked and the shift lever must be in
neutral.
Do not touch any components of the ignition system after the engine is started.
When the engine is running, hands, clothes, tools and other objects shall not contact cooling fan, drive belt and other rotating parts.
Do not touch any heat removal parts, such as exhaust manifold, A/C high-pressure pipe, muffler, etc. to avoid burns.
Do not make any conductive objects close to the positive and negative poles of the battery to avoid short circuit, resulting in battery
damage, fire, explosion and other accidents.
Do not discharge any liquid on the vehicle at will, such as gasoline, engine oil, coolant, brake fluid, power steering fluid (if equipped),
etc. These liquids pollute the environment.
Prevent water from entering the control unit during cleaning and maintenance.
For electronic throttle valve faults, do not disassemble it for repair.
The fuel system maintains high-pressure gasoline in the fuel circuit even if the engine is not running. Therefore, the replacement of
fuel pipe and fuel filter should be carried out in a well-ventilated place.
The ECU temperature shall not exceed 80 ° C in the event of a thermal simulation of the fault or a service operation that may
increase the vehicle temperature.
When disconnecting the harness connector of the control module, the ignition switch must be turned to the "OFF" position.
Equipment that may cause electromagnetic interference should not be installed near the ECU to avoid interference.
When welding the vehicle, disconnect the harness connector of each control module and disconnect all battery connection
harnesses.
